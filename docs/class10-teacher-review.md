# Class 10 — points for teachers to check

Every Class 10 lesson was written to the chapter-kit standard, then checked by an
independent AI reviewer (answers recomputed, Java run, equations balanced). These are the
points the writers and reviewers were **not fully sure of**. None is known to be wrong;
each needs a teacher's yes / no against the current textbook.

Boards: CBSE (NCERT), ICSE (CISCE), Karnataka SSLC (KSEAB).

## Syllabus coverage (Karnataka SSLC especially)
- Chapter lists were made from known syllabi, not the current printed books. Please compare
  each board's Class 10 list (Library → Classes 9–10 → board) with this year's syllabus.
- Karnataka chapters written from general knowledge: *Political Developments of the 20th
  Century*, *International Institutions* (linked to the UN lessons only; add SAARC /
  Commonwealth if the book has them), *Problems of India and their Solutions*,
  *India's Relationship with Other Countries*, *Global Problems and India's Role*, the four
  Sociology chapters, *Globalisation of Business*, *Consumer Education and Protection*.
- Karnataka geography groupings: four physiographic divisions, six soil types, seasons named
  winter / summer / south-west monsoon / retreating monsoon.
- Anglo-Mysore wars are taught under *The Extension of the British Rule*.

## History
- James Watt's improved steam engine: dated **1781** as in NCERT (other sources: 1769 patent).
- Diamond Sutra (868) called "the oldest Japanese book" as in NCERT (historically Chinese).
- English first factory "at Surat (1613)" as in textbooks (Masulipatnam 1611 was earlier).
- Aga Khan as "first president" of the Muslim League (honorary/permanent president).
- Versailles reparations "£6,600 million (1921)" as in ICSE books.
- Karnataka resistance: Amara Sullia (1837; Kalyanaswamy, Puttabasappa), Dhondiya Wagh
  (killed 1800), Nargund Baba Saheb's grievance.
- MAO school 1875 (college 1877); "more than 550" princely states; Bose's death "believed".

## Geography and civics
- ICSE toposheet colours (grid lines red, spot heights black) and 20 m contour interval.
- Census 2011 figures are used throughout (and labelled as such).
- Lok Sabha: max 550 elected, Anglo-Indian seats ended 2020; present strength 543 / 245.
- Supreme Court: sanctioned strength 34 since 2019.
- Women's reservation amendment (2023) described as taking effect after census and delimitation.
- Appiko (1983, Salkani, Pandurang Hegde); KRRS (1980, M. D. Nanjundaswamy); SAARC details.

## Economics and business studies
- Consumer commissions: no money limits given (they were revised); complaint within 2 years.
- Cheque validity 3 months; Stree Shakti described without a start year.
- MUDRA: Shishu / Kishore / Tarun (+ Tarun Plus added later).
- Cheque-writing "order" activity: the order is a convention, not a rule.

## English
- Board formats and marks: CBSE letter / analytical paragraph 5 marks, 100–120 words;
  ICSE composition 20 marks, 300–350 words; ICSE letter 10 marks; notice + e-mail ~50 words.
- ICSE formal letter: the lesson puts the subject before the salutation (CBSE order).
- Usage choices: "Of the two sisters, Leela is the taller"; "must → had to (or must)";
  collective nouns with plural verbs; "Bread and butter has".

## Hindi
- Letter format order: परीक्षा भवन + दिनांक → सेवा में → विषय → महोदय → … → भवदीय.
  The order activity treats this as the only correct order.
- 'ने' rule is simplified (exceptions such as नहाना, छींकना, लाना are not covered).
- डरना + से taught as अपादान; 'क्योंकि' → मिश्र, 'इसलिए' → संयुक्त; महात्मा as कर्मधारय;
  चतुर्भुज as बहुव्रीहि; "पढ़ता होगा" as संदिग्ध वर्तमान; ज़ीरा with nukta.

## Kannada
- ಗಮಕ ಸಮಾಸ examples (ಆ ಹುಡುಗ, ಮಾಡಿದಡಿಗೆ, ಸುಟ್ಟಿಟ್ಟಿಗೆ) and the simplified definition.
- ಅಂಶಿ ಸಮಾಸ examples (ಮುಂಗೈ, ಮುಂದಲೆ, ಅಂಗೈ, ತುದಿಬೆರಳು); ಮುಕ್ಕಣ್ಣ as ಬಹುವ್ರೀಹಿ.
- ಆಗಮ ಸಂಧಿ rule simplified; ಔ not listed for ವಕಾರಾಗಮ.
- ಇಂದ always ತೃತೀಯಾ, ದೆಸೆಯಿಂದ ಪಂಚಮೀ; ಸಂಬೋಧನಾ endings ಆ / ಏ / ಓ.
- ನೀನು / ನೀವು verb forms: ಓದುತ್ತೀಯೆ, ಓದಿದೆ, ಓದುವೆ, ಓದುತ್ತೀರಿ, ಓದಿದಿರಿ, ಓದುವಿರಿ.
- Only the three main genders are taught (add ಪುಂಸ್ತ್ರೀಲಿಂಗ etc. if the board needs them).
- ತತ್ಸಮ–ತದ್ಭವ: ಸ್ತಂಭ–ಕಂಬ, ಪುಸ್ತಕ–ಹೊತ್ತಿಗೆ, ಆಶ್ಚರ್ಯ–ಅಚ್ಚರಿ, ಮುಖ–ಮೊಗ.
- ಅಲಂಕಾರ answer format and the five alankaras taught; ಉತ್ಪ್ರೇಕ್ಷೆ marker "ಓ ಎಂಬಂತೆ".
- Letter format (place / date position) and "sandhi question is usually 1 mark".

## Computer applications
- HTML: deprecated attributes (`type` on lists, `border`, `cellpadding`) taught as in the
  CBSE book. Telnet expanded as "Teletype Network".
- Java: "constructor usually public, cannot be static"; default values when no constructor.

## Maths and science
- GST: the September 2025 slab change (5% / 18%, 40% for luxury goods) is mentioned;
  lessons tell students to use the rate given in the question.
- EM spectrum boundaries (microwave / radio); "10 million carbon compounds" (NCERT ~3 million);
  marriage age 18 / 21; cytokinins listed as breaking dormancy.

## General knowledge
- Silchar Polo Club (1859); snakes and ladders from Moksha Patam; chaturanga by the
  6th century CE; Kalinga War c. 261 BCE; Brihadeeswarar completed c. 1010; Khonoma as
  "first green village"; Durand Cup (1888); India has no official national game (2012 RTI).
- Eight classical dances (Sangeet Natak Akademi; the Ministry of Culture lists nine).
